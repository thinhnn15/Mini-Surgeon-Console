# Risk table (mini FMEA)

Modelled loosely on ISO 14971: hazard, how it happens, the harm, the control, and how the control
is verified. Severity and probability are rough (1 = low, 5 = high).

| ID | Hazard | Sequence of events | Harm | Sev | Prob | Control | Verified by | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Arm keeps moving on an old command | Network stalls, `arm_sim` keeps applying the last command | Tissue injury | 5 | 3 | Heartbeat watchdog in `arm_sim`, hold position after 3 missed periods | `arm_sim.REQ_SAF_001_HoldsWhenTheConsoleDies`, `console.REQ_SAF_002_EveryFaultEndsInASafeState` ("cable pulled") | REQ-SAF-001 |
| RISK-002 | Overlay shows the instrument in the wrong place | Telemetry arrives late, overlay drawn with old data | Wrong judgement of position | 4 | 3 | Timestamp every sample, hide overlay when stale | | REQ-HUD-001 |
| RISK-003 | Video lags behind the hands | Frames queue up between capture and display | Imprecise motion | 4 | 3 | Latest-frame mailbox, latency measured in CI | | REQ-VID-001 |
| RISK-004 | A frozen picture looks live | Camera unplugged or its driver stalls; the renderer keeps drawing the last frame, which looks exactly like live video | Surgeon acts on where the instruments were, not where they are | 5 | 3 | Watch the age of the newest frame on screen (capture timestamp), on the GUI thread independently of source and renderer; red "VIDEO FROZEN" banner after 250 ms | `console_app.REQ_VID_003_FrozenVideoIsFlagged` | REQ-VID-003 |
| RISK-005 | The arm follows a hand reading that stopped updating | Hand controller freezes or is unplugged; the last pose stays "valid" | Unintended motion | 4 | 2 | Hand-input watchdog (100 ms) in the teleop core, fault and hold | `Fixture.REQ_SAF_002_StaleHandInputFaultsAfterItsTimeout`, `console.REQ_SAF_002_EveryFaultEndsInASafeState` ("hand controller frozen", detected in ~105 ms) | REQ-SAF-002 |
| RISK-006 | Commands do not reach the arm, and the console does not know | Corruption on the wire, the arm refusing commands; the console keeps believing it drives the arm | Operator acts on a model of the arm that is wrong | 4 | 2 | CRC-32 (the arm refuses), the arm acknowledges applied commands only, acknowledgement watchdog in every state, reset needs an acknowledgement newer than the fault | `Fixture.REQ_SAF_002_CommandsNotAcknowledgedFault`, `Fixture.REQ_SAF_002_ResetIsRefusedWhileTheArmStillDoesNotAcknowledge`, `console.REQ_SAF_002_EveryFaultEndsInASafeState` ("corrupted commands") | REQ-COM-001, REQ-SAF-002 |
| RISK-007 | The arm is moved while the surgeon cannot see | Video freezes or never starts while engaged | Instrument moves blind | 5 | 2 | Video not live (REQ-VID-003) blocks engaging and faults an engaged console | `Fixture.REQ_SAF_002_VideoFrozenWhileEngagedFaults`, `console.REQ_SAF_002_EveryFaultEndsInASafeState` ("video not live") | REQ-VID-003, REQ-SAF-002 |
| RISK-008 | Motion restarts by itself after a fault | The cause goes away (a link that flickers back) and tele-operation resumes | Unexpected motion with the operator not ready | 5 | 2 | Fault latched; reset refused while a cause is present; reset leads to Idle, never Engaged; the arm holds until a new command | `StateMachine.REQ_SAF_002_*`, `ArmController.REQ_SAF_001_WhenTheConsoleComesBackItWaitsForANewCommand` | REQ-SAF-002 |
